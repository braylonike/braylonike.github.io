About Me

Hi, my name is Braylon Ike, and I am currently a sophomore at Cal Poly Pomona majoring in Mechanical Engineering, if you couldn't already tell. I wanted to include this section because I feel like just reading a resume doesn't give you a clear picture of who I am as a person, so here's a little more about me.

I grew up in Rancho Cucamonga, California. It's a fairly quiet place without much going on, but I liked to make the most of it. The way I did that was by working with my hands. I've always been interested in how things work, so when I was a kid I would take things apart just to see what was inside. Funny story, I once tried taking apart my Nintendo 3DS and messed up a ribbon cable, so I couldn't put it back together. I still have it in pieces to this day.

As I got older and started making some money, I became a lot less destructive with that curiosity. I started working on my car and building small projects instead. That habit of taking things apart just to understand them eventually turned into a desire to make my own things, which is what led me to engineering.

At first, I was really interested in nuclear energy because I saw it as a promising way to make our power grid more sustainable, and I found how it worked fascinating. Over time, as I learned more about different fields, I decided to study mechanical engineering instead. It's a broad major that gives me the flexibility to explore many areas, from renewable energy and energy storage to project management. I like the idea of being able to see my contributions from both a hands-on and big-picture perspective.

Outside of school, I enjoy hiking, camping, and spending time with friends. I like being outdoors and finding a good balance between learning, building, and relaxing.
