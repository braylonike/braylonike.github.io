01 / About Me

My Relationship with Engineering

I have been interested in engineering since I was young, but what really got me into it was researching nuclear reactors in high school. I found the whole concept fascinating, and I became a little obsessed with learning how they worked. Eventually, I started thinking, “Wow, I really want to design those.”

My original plan was to pursue nuclear engineering, possibly through the Navy. After giving it a lot of thought, I decided to go to college instead. Since there were not many nuclear engineering programs available, I chose mechanical engineering because it would still allow me to work in the nuclear field while giving me the flexibility to explore other areas.

At first, I thought of mechanical engineering as a general path that could eventually lead me back to nuclear reactors. However, as I began working on cars and learning more about mechanical systems, I started enjoying the hands-on side of engineering more than I expected. My coursework at Cal Poly Pomona reinforced that interest and helped me realize that choosing mechanical engineering was the right decision for me.

I have grown a lot at Cal Poly, both personally and technically. Early in my first semester, I worked on a complicated reverse-engineering project that helped me become much more comfortable with CAD. I later taught myself more about GD&T, worked with MATLAB, and began applying those skills to projects of my own. Cal Poly's “Learn by Doing” approach has been especially important to me because it gave me the opportunity to build things, solve problems, and learn from the process instead of only studying concepts in a classroom.

As I have continued developing my skills, I have become more interested in actually building and improving mechanical systems. Working on my own projects has broadened my interest in engineering and made me excited to eventually join the industry through an internship or co-op. I am still interested in energy and nuclear engineering, but I am also grateful that mechanical engineering has given me the flexibility to discover how much I enjoy hands-on design and mechanical systems.
